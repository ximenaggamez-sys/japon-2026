富士山

Going to Mt Fuji

Base yourself at Lake Kawaguchi (Kawaguchiko), the Fuji Five Lakes town on the mountain's north side, about 2 h from Shinjuku.

When to go

Late November is one of the clearest times of year and the summit is usually snow-capped. Go early: clouds often build by midday. Check the forecast the night before and keep the day flexible: Nov 12, 13 or 15, or Dec 3–4 (Nov 14 is Nikko and Dec 2 is Kamakura).

Best views

Chureito Pagoda (Shimoyoshida station, 398 steps), Oishi Park on the north shore, the Kachi Kachi Ropeway up Mt Tenjo, the spring ponds of Oshino Hakkai, and Lake Yamanaka.

5th Station & climbing

Climbing is only allowed July to September. The Subaru Line road and buses up to the 5th Station (2,300 m) run only when there's no snow or ice, so check that morning.

Getting around

Fujikyu's retro sightseeing buses loop Lake Kawaguchi from Kawaguchiko Station; a 1- or 2-day pass covers them. The Fujikyu Line links Kawaguchiko, Fuji-Q Highland and Shimoyoshida.

From Hakone

Buses via Gotemba link Lake Ashi and Lake Kawaguchi in about 2 h with a change. It's easier to do Fuji as its own day from Tokyo; on a clear day you'll also see it from Lake Ashi on Nov 16.

From the Shinkansen

Between Tokyo and Osaka, window seat E faces Mt Fuji in both directions, around Shin-Fuji station.

Step by step: lines, stations and transfers

For each leg: where to board, which line and train to take, where to change, and where you come out. Red names are what the station signs say in Japanese. Tap an option to open it.

Two kinds of ticket

Metro, local JR, Hankyu, Kintetsu, Odakyu and city buses: just tap Suica/ICOCA in and out. Shinkansen and limited expresses need a seat ticket. Buy it at the green JR machines (みどりの券売機) or online (smartEX for the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen, JR-WEST Online, JR-EAST Train Reservation), and feed all tickets into the gate together.

Unreserved cars

On Nozomi, Mizuho and Sakura trains, cars 1–3 are unreserved. On Hikari and Kodama it's usually cars 1–5. Line up at the painted marks on the platform floor that show your car number.

Live platforms

Platform numbers can change by train. Check the departure boards (they alternate Japanese and English) or Google Maps / Navitime on the day.

Last trains

Most city lines stop between midnight and 0:30 and restart around 5:00. After that, it's taxi only, with a 20% late-night surcharge from about 22:00 to 5:00.

Seats for a group

Unreserved Shinkansen cars are fine for 1–3 people on weekdays. For 5 or 8 to sit together, especially on weekends and Nov 23, reserve; it adds about ¥300–900 per person.

Big suitcases

On the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen, bags over 160 cm (length + width + height) need a seat with an oversized-baggage area booked in advance. Sending bags ahead by takkyubin is easier.

Taxi or Uber

Uber and GO call regular metered taxis, 24 hours a day, so the price is the meter plus a small booking fee. A taxi seats 4, so 5 or 8 people need two cars or a jumbo taxi (about 20–30% more).

Metro and local trains

Add Suica or ICOCA to your phone's wallet; it works on subways, JR local trains, Hankyu, Kintetsu, Odakyu and city buses. Limited-express surcharges and Shinkansen need a separate ticket.
